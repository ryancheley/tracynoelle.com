# tracynoelle.com

Portfolio and contact site for **Tracy Noelle Zimmerman**, a private chef
specializing in plant-based cuisine. The site acts as a digital business card
and inquiry hub for prospective clients.

## About Tracy

A private chef with 25+ years in the culinary field, Tracy has lived a
plant-based lifestyle for 15+ years and holds culinary and nutrition
certifications from Tante Marie Cooking School (San Francisco) and Sterling
College (Sydney), plus a master's in Psychology. She co-authored the vegan
cookbook *The Compassionate Kitchen* and founded **Shefdress**, a tailored
chef-wear brand for women in professional kitchens. She is based in Palm
Springs.

## Pages

- **Home** — branding and headshot, tagline "Private Chef ~ Plant Based"
- **About** — background, certifications, and philosophy
- **Contact** — inquiries via email (tracy@tracynoelle.com)
