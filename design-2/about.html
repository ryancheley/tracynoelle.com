About

Tracy Noelle Zimmerman

The Chef

Tracy Noelle Zimmerman is a vegan private chef based in the Palm Springs area, bringing plant-based fine dining to homes and gatherings across the Coachella Valley. With more than twenty-five years in the culinary field and over fifteen years living plant-based, her cooking is the work of a lifetime spent learning what vegetables can truly become.

Her style is quiet and considered: seasonal produce, clean technique, and plates that feel like restraint rather than abundance. She co-authored the vegan cookbook The Compassionate Kitchen and founded Shefdress, a chef-wear brand designed for women in professional kitchens.

A master's in Psychology informs the way she hosts — attentive to the room, the pacing of a meal, and the small details that make guests feel genuinely cared for.

Philosophy

Plant-based, without compromise.

Vegan food, done well, needs no apology and no imitation. Tracy builds menus from the desert's seasons — citrus and dates in winter, stone fruit and tomatoes in summer — letting each ingredient stand on its own. The result is food that is compassionate by nature and luxurious by design.
